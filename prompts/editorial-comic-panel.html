Editorial comic panel

Compose one energetic retro comic panel with punchy colors, halftone texture, and expressive inked lines.

Copy this prompt

A single comic-book panel drawn in a bold mid-century pulp-adventure style: dynamic diagonal composition, halftone shading, a punchy limited color palette of red, cream, and navy, and one short exclamatory caption box in the corner. Thick expressive linework around the main figure.

How to use it

  1. Describe the character and action you want in the panel.
  2. Keep captions brief, and verify any generated text before use.
  3. Adjust the palette and composition to suit your story.
